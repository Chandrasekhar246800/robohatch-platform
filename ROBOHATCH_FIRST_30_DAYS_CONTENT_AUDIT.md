# RoboHatch First 30 Days Content Audit

## Scope
Part 3 of the launch identity plan: 30 Instagram reel ideas and a content system focused on trust, conversion, manufacturing proof, creator communities, and first-customer acquisition.

## Content Goals for First 30 Days
- Build trust fast
- Show manufacturing credibility
- Make the brand memorable
- Drive first purchases and uploads
- Collect early UGC and testimonials
- Create repeatable short-form content pillars

## Content Pillars
1. Product showcase
2. Manufacturing behind-the-scenes
3. Creator economy use cases
4. Rapid prototyping / engineering proof
5. Packaging and delivery trust
6. Customer trust and testimonials

## 30 Reel Ideas

### Week 1: Brand introduction and trust
1. **RoboHatch is live**
   - Reveal the brand and what it solves
   - CTA: shop / upload

2. **From file to physical product**
   - Show the full transformation sequence
   - CTA: upload your design

3. **Why engineer QA matters**
   - Explain DFM and tolerance checks simply
   - CTA: trust the process

4. **Secure checkout with Razorpay**
   - Show payment confidence and support details
   - CTA: buy safely

5. **What makes a 3D print premium**
   - Surface finish, fit, material, post-processing
   - CTA: compare quality

### Week 2: Product and manufacturing proof
6. **Printer timelapse of a clean print**
   - Fast, satisfying, visually strong
   - CTA: explore products

7. **Packaging an order for shipment**
   - Bubble wrap, boxes, tracking, care
   - CTA: order with confidence

8. **Quality check table reveal**
   - Show inspection and verification
   - CTA: engineer-reviewed output

9. **Best-selling product spotlight**
   - One product, one use case, one CTA
   - CTA: shop now

10. **How we handle custom uploads**
   - File received, reviewed, printed, delivered
   - CTA: upload your file

### Week 3: Creator economy and utility
11. **Desk setup upgrade**
   - A printed object in a real workspace
   - CTA: personalize your desk

12. **Gift idea reel**
   - Show premium gifting use case
   - CTA: order for a friend

13. **Prototype to product**
   - Turn an idea into a physical object
   - CTA: rapid prototyping

14. **How creators can monetize merch**
   - Explain small-batch product opportunities
   - CTA: custom orders

15. **Color and finish comparison**
   - Compare materials, colors, and textures
   - CTA: choose your style

### Week 4: Trust, proof, and conversion
16. **Customer quote on screen**
   - Strong testimonial style reel
   - CTA: shop with confidence

17. **What happens after you order**
   - Order received → production → QC → shipping
   - CTA: know the process

18. **WhatsApp support in action**
   - Show human support and response speed
   - CTA: ask a question

19. **Insured shipping explained**
   - Reassure first-time buyers

20. **Refund policy in plain language**
   - Short, clear, confidence-building

## Weekly Posting Rhythm
- 5 reels per week
- 2 story sequences per week
- 1 carousel per week
- 1 testimonial or UGC asset per week

## High-Engagement Formats
- Before/after transformation
- Timelapse
- Close-up satisfying process shots
- Founder on camera
- Customer reaction / unboxing
- Simple question-answer clips

## Trust-Building Content
- Shipping proof
- Packaging proof
- QA proof
- Customer support proof
- Payment security proof
- Refund clarity proof

## Conversion-Focused Content
Every piece of content should end with one of these CTAs:
- Shop products
- Upload your design
- WhatsApp us
- Read FAQ
- See how it works

## Early-Customer Acquisition Angles
- Engineering communities
- Maker communities
- College clubs
- Small business owners
- Custom gift buyers
- Rapid prototyping users

## UGC Collection Strategy
- Ask every satisfied customer for:
  - a photo of the product on their desk
  - a short unboxing clip
  - one sentence review
- Offer a feature on Instagram in return
- Keep review requests short and direct

## Launch Content QA
- Show real products, not mockups
- Use the same brand palette as the website
- Keep captions short and benefit-focused
- Use the same support email / WhatsApp everywhere
- Avoid exaggerated claims
- Keep the visual language premium and engineering-led
