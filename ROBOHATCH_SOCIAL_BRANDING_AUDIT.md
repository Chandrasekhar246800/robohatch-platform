# RoboHatch Social Branding Audit

## Scope
Part 2 of the launch identity plan: Instagram profile, pinned content, highlight structure, launch post strategy, and creator-facing storytelling.

## Brand Positioning
RoboHatch should present as an engineering-first, creator-friendly, premium Indian manufacturing brand.

### Positioning statement
Precision 3D printing and custom manufacturing for creators, founders, and product teams who want reliable output, secure checkout, and production-grade support.

### Tone of voice
- Confident, technical, and calm
- Premium but not corporate
- Maker-friendly, not overly salesy
- Indian startup credibility with manufacturing discipline

## Instagram Bio
Recommended bio:

Premium 3D printing in India
Engineer-reviewed • Custom & ready-made
Razorpay-secure checkout
Upload designs or shop bestsellers
WhatsApp support available

### Alternate shorter bio
Engineered 3D prints for creators
Custom manufacturing in India
Secure payments • Insured shipping

## Profile Description
- Profile name: RoboHatch
- Category: Shopping & retail / Manufacturing
- Bio link: landing page or link hub with Shop, Upload Design, FAQ, Contact
- Profile photo: simplified brand mark or sharp wordmark

## Pinned Post Strategy
Pin three posts only:

1. **What RoboHatch does**
   - Explain the brand in one clean post
   - Show product range, custom uploads, secure payment, and shipping
   - CTA: Shop products / Upload a design

2. **How production works**
   - Visual walkthrough of file upload → DFM check → print → QA → packaging → delivery
   - CTA: Trust the process

3. **Why RoboHatch is different**
   - Engineer QA, premium materials, insured shipping, Razorpay checkout, WhatsApp support
   - CTA: First order / first collaboration

## Highlight Categories
Recommended Instagram highlight structure:

- **Start Here**: intro to brand and products
- **Process**: upload, print, QA, packaging
- **Reviews**: customer testimonials and UGC
- **Shipping**: packing, dispatch, tracking
- **Custom**: uploads, prototypes, personalized items
- **Support**: WhatsApp, FAQs, response times
- **Materials**: PLA, PETG, and finish options
- **Behind Scenes**: workshop and printer footage

## Launch Post Ideas
Launch sequence:

### Post 1: Brand reveal
- Message: RoboHatch is now live
- Visual: premium hero shot of printed parts / workshop / product grid
- CTA: shop or upload

### Post 2: Process trust post
- Message: engineer-reviewed production and QA
- Visual: checklist, printer, post-processing, packaging
- CTA: see how it works

### Post 3: Secure checkout post
- Message: Razorpay-backed payments and support
- Visual: minimal trust graphic with badges
- CTA: browse products

### Post 4: Social proof post
- Message: early customer outcomes and feedback
- Visual: customer quote cards or UGC carousel
- CTA: buy with confidence

### Post 5: Creator economy post
- Message: creators can turn ideas into products
- Visual: desk setup, custom keychain, prototype, figurine
- CTA: upload your own design

## Creator-Focused Content Strategy
### Content pillars
1. **Product showcase**
   - high-quality stills and motion
   - close-ups of print texture and finish

2. **Manufacturing proof**
   - printer timelapses
   - QA and inspection shots
   - packaging and shipment prep

3. **Creator utility**
   - how a design becomes a physical object
   - rapid prototyping use cases
   - personalized merchandise

4. **Trust and legitimacy**
   - founder-led story
   - secure payment messaging
   - shipping and refund clarity

5. **Community and UGC**
   - customer unboxings
   - desk setup shots
   - before/after design transformations

## Social Identity Rules
- Use the same orange, slate, and steel palette as the website
- Keep layouts airy and premium
- Use short captions with one primary CTA
- Show real product and manufacturing visuals more than abstract graphics
- Keep engineering details visible, but simple enough for casual buyers
